### SUN THERAPY

Opening event on Friday, 27 November, 4:00-6:00 PM.

***SUN THERAPY*** explores the relationship between light, the human body, and seasonal changes. During winter, reduced daylight can disrupt our circadian rhythm, affect sleep and mood, and contribute to seasonal affective disorder (SAD), commonly known as winter depression.

The project explores light therapy as a way to counteract the effects of sun deprivation during the darkest months of the year. *SUN THERAPY* creates a space for contemplation, healing, and restoration, reflecting on our vital need for sunlight and its influence on the body and mind.

●	*Heliophile (noun) is an organism, plant, or person that is strongly attracted to, or thrives in, sunlight. The term originates from the Greek words helios (sun) and philos (loving).*

### About the Artist

Maria Pita Guerreiro is a Portuguese experimental designer and artist based in Stockholm. Her work sits at the intersection of design, art, craft, science, and health, drawing inspiration from natural systems, rituals, the human body, and feminism. She primarily focuses on material research, exploring the tension between disgust and attraction through living materials such as fungi and bacteria.

She has participated in numerous art and design residencies throughout Europe, and her work has been featured internationally at design fairs, museums, and galleries. Her most recent solo show, WINTER BLUES, was exhibited at Konstepidemin in Gothenburg.
