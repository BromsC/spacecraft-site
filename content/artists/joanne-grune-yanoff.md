Welcome to the opening with performance on Friday 4 December.\
4:00-5:00 PM

*Cassandra’s FlightBox* is a solo exhibition by Joanne Grüne-Yanoff, which includes videos, objects, and a short gestural play, which will be performed throughout the week by Swedish actor Johannes Jonhstrom.

Cassandra, a recurring character around which Grüne-Yanoff’s practice centers, is a grounded being who watches the skies, thinking about flight. Cassandra doesn’t want to fly; they want to imagine flying, and with it, a sense of space, freedom, possibility.

*Cassandra’s FlightBox* will contain a series of videos of different Cassandras in action – jumping and flapping. Along with the videos, some of Cassandra’s props will sit on the walls and pedastals, among them a jumpsuit, an eggshell helmet,  and some necklaces – props that Cassandra uses to find a sense of transformation. During performances, a live Cassandra, played by Johannes Jonhstrom, will walk through the space, interacting with the works, and adding another layer to the story of *Cassandra’s FlightBox*.
